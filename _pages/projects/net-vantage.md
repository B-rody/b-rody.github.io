## Summary
Net Vantage is a Shopify app for B2B and wholesale merchants that need stronger control over customer credit and payment-terms orders. I independently designed, built, and launched the product through Valence Core, the studio and publisher behind the app.

The product brings credit policies, accounts-receivable visibility, and order safeguards into the merchant's Shopify workflow so teams can identify exposure and enforce purchasing rules without relying on disconnected spreadsheets.

## Role & Scope
I owned Net Vantage end to end: product strategy, requirements, user workflows, system architecture, Shopify integrations and extensions, implementation, testing, deployment, and launch. That included translating wholesale credit-management needs into a focused product, designing the merchant and buyer experiences, and operating the release path from development through Shopify App Store publication.

## Product Capabilities
- **Customer and company credit limits:** Configure and monitor credit exposure at the buyer or B2B company level.
- **A/R dashboard and aging buckets:** Review outstanding balances grouped by aging period to prioritize follow-up.
- **Payment-terms order tracking:** Track orders placed on payment terms from a centralized operational view.
- **Draft-order warnings:** Surface credit-risk context before staff complete draft-order workflows.
- **B2B checkout restriction and blocking:** Enforce credit policies during checkout when a buyer exceeds the configured limit.
- **Reliability metrics:** Review each customer's paid count, late count, and average days late.
- **CSV export:** Export operational credit and receivables data for reporting or downstream workflows.

## Product & Engineering Approach
Net Vantage coordinates data and workflows across Shopify's B2B, order, customer, company, draft-order, and checkout surfaces. The merchant experience centralizes credit configuration and receivables oversight, while Shopify extensions place warnings and enforcement where purchasing decisions happen.

Testing covered credit calculations, payment-term states, warning and blocking behavior, extension workflows, and failure cases. Deployment and launch work included production configuration, operational readiness, and the Shopify App Store release process.
